# Design System: College Search & Intelligence Platform

## Concept
A professional, academic, and highly trustworthy UI for a college document search engine. It should feel robust, clean, and modern. Avoid generic "AI tech" colors (no bright purples or neons). Use a serious, academic palette.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Navy Blue (`#1E3A8A`) - Used for headers, primary buttons, and active states.
- **Secondary**: Slate Gray (`#475569`) - Used for secondary buttons, borders, and muted text.
- **Accent**: Subtle Gold (`#D4AF37`) - Used sparingly for highlights, notifications, or special badges (e.g., "AI Answer" badge).
- **Background**: Off-White (`#F8FAFC`) - For app backgrounds, ensuring high contrast with text.
- **Surface**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) - For cards, modals, and search bars.

## Typography
- Use a clean sans-serif font like Inter or Roboto.
- Headers should be bold and authoritative.
- Body text should be highly legible for reading long academic texts.

## UI Elements
- **Cards**: Use soft, subtle shadows (keep document cards clean and flat with slight rounded corners `rounded-lg`).
- **Search Bar**: Large, prominent, and centered on the home page with a soft drop shadow.
- **Buttons**: Slightly rounded, solid colors.
